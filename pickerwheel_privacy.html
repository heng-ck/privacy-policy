Picker Wheel - Choice Roulette

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Picker Wheel - Choice Roulette, developed by AppMe7, handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: October 30, 2026
Application ID: com.appme7.pickerwheel

1. About the app

Picker Wheel - Choice Roulette is an interactive decision-making tool that lets you create custom spinning wheels, set choice probabilities, randomize outcomes, and play party games.

2. Information we access or collect

Picker Wheel is built with a local-first architecture. All your custom wheels, titles, choices, slice weights, and spin history are stored exclusively on your device's internal local storage. We do not require registration, accounts, or personal information.

3. How information is used

Your local wheel data is used solely to render your customized roulette wheels and display your past spin outcomes within the app.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Your data stays on your device. We do not transmit, sell, rent, or share your wheels or spin outcomes with external servers or third parties.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising identifiers (AD_ID), and coarse usage statistics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies

6. Permissions and your choices

The app requests the following system permissions:

  • VIBRATE: Used to provide realistic haptic feedback when the pointer hits wheel sectors and upon winning. You can disable vibration at any time in the app settings.
  • INTERNET: Used to display advertisements and open official Google Play or Galaxy Store listings when you choose to share or rate the app.

7. Retention and deletion

All data is retained locally on your device until you delete a wheel, clear history, or uninstall the application. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data.

8. Children's privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

9. Security

We prioritize your privacy by keeping your data locally on your device, safeguarded by standard Android app sandboxing.
