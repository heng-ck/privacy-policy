Connect Dots: Color Match Game

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Connect Dots: Color Match Game, provided by CKH, handles information when you play the game.

Effective date: 2026-11-25
Application ID: com.gameme7.connectdotscolormatchgame

1. About the game

Connect Dots: Color Match Game is a single-player puzzle game. You drag through adjacent dots of the same color to clear them, complete level goals within a move limit, and use Rocket, Bomb and Shuffle boosters across 300 levels. The game needs no account or login, offers no in-app purchases, and can be played without an internet connection. An internet connection is used only to load ads and the advertising consent form.

2. Local game data and information accessed

Your game progress is stored only on your device using Android SharedPreferences. It includes unlocked and last completed level, stars and best score per level, total score, cleared-level count, booster counts (Rocket, Bomb, Shuffle), which tutorial popups you have seen, your daily reward streak and last claim date, rating-prompt milestones, and your Sound, Music and Vibration settings. This data is never uploaded to our servers. We do not operate any servers for this game and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or other personal files.

3. How information is used

Local game data is used only to save your progress, resume your next level, apply your audio and vibration preferences, and run the daily reward calendar. Information processed by Google's advertising services (described below) is used to show, measure and limit ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to remember your advertising consent choices.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Game progress stays on your device. We do not sell, rent or share it. The advertising SDK included in the game may send device and usage information directly from your device to Google over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. That processing may take place on Google servers in other countries, under Google's own privacy terms.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob: a 320×50 banner shown in its own strip at the bottom of the screen, interstitial ads shown only after completing certain levels, and optional rewarded videos (for example "+5 Moves", "+3 boosters" or a doubled daily reward) that you choose to watch. Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, app interaction and ad engagement data, and diagnostics in order to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice later from Ad Privacy Choices in the game's Settings when this option is shown.

6. Permissions and your privacy choices

The game requests only these Android permissions: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to load ads and the consent form), VIBRATE (optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings), and AD_ID (Google Advertising ID for ads). The ads SDK also adds ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS (Android Privacy Sandbox ad services) and WAKE_LOCK (used by the SDK's background ad tasks). The game does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or a foreground service. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your device's Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Ads), and you can update your consent with Ad Privacy Choices in the game when available.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game progress is kept on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the game. Because we have no accounts and no servers, there is no copy of your progress held by us. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies linked above.

8. Children's privacy

This game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help to address it.

9. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Communication with Google's advertising services uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the data we handle to the minimum needed for the game to work.

10. Privacy requests

You can exercise your choices directly on your device: clear app data or uninstall to remove local progress, use Ad Privacy Choices in the game when shown, and manage your Advertising ID in Android settings. For requests about data processed by Google, please use Google's privacy tools. For any other questions or requests about this game, contact us at the email below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or the services it uses change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Please review it from time to time.
