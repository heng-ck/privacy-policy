Save Location GPS & Live Share

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Save Location GPS & Live Share, provided by CKH, handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: October 30, 2026
Application ID: com.savelocation.gpsshare

1. About the app

Save Location GPS & Live Share is an offline-first personal location bookmarking, trip tracking, and navigation assistant. The application enables users to quickly capture high-precision GPS coordinates, record notes, view custom canvas maps, use a compass-guided walking radar HUD, and share live location progress temporarily with trusted contacts.

2. Information we access or collect

We access and collect only the information strictly necessary to provide the features you explicitly use:

  • Precise and Approximate Location (GPS and Network Coordinates): Accessed when you view the map, bookmark a location, use the walking radar, or activate a live sharing session.
  • User-Created Notes and Labels: Custom titles, descriptions, and categories you attach to saved places.
  • Selected Photographs: When you choose to attach a photo (such as a parking column marker) using the system photo picker, the photo URI is referenced locally. The app does not scan or upload your general photo library.

3. How information is used

All location and bookmarking data is processed solely for core app functionalities requested by you:

  • Displaying your current spot on the interactive map canvas.
  • Calculating relative heading azimuth and step distance in the Walking Radar HUD.
  • Broadcasting temporary location updates during an active Live Sharing session.
  • Exporting and importing data in CSV, GPX, and KML formats at your direct command.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Save Location GPS & Live Share is designed with an Offline-First architecture. All your bookmarked coordinates, categories, and notes are saved directly to your device's private local storage. No data is stored on remote servers by default. Data is only shared externally when you intentionally tap to share via third-party messaging apps (e.g. WhatsApp, LINE), launch navigation intents, or present an on-screen QR code for nearby scanning.

Zero Sale of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or location data to data brokers, advertisers, or analytics corporations.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The app integrates standard Android platform services to provide reverse geocoding and external map links:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

This application contains zero third-party advertising SDKs and zero user tracking beacons.

6. Permissions and your choices

The app requests the following Android runtime permissions with full user transparency:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Required to pinpoint current GPS coordinates and calculate radar directions.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION and FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Required only during an active Live Share session to transmit location updates with an ongoing status bar notification. Users can tap "Stop Sharing" at any time.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display the ongoing Live Share status and timer.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your Android device's Settings > Apps > Save Location GPS & Live Share > Permissions.

7. Retention and deletion

You have complete control over your data. Saved spots remain on your device until you edit or delete them. You can delete individual locations or clear all application data at any time via the app settings or Android App Storage settings. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all locally stored coordinates and notes.

8. Children's privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal or location information from children under the age of 13, in strict adherence to COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and global privacy standards.

9. Security

We apply rigorous security practices to protect your data. All database records reside inside the protected Android application sandbox. Any network communication (such as reverse geocoding lookup) is conducted strictly over encrypted HTTPS/TLS channels.

10. Privacy requests

Under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to access, export, and delete your personal information. Because our application does not maintain centralized user accounts, your entire dataset is directly accessible and exportable as CSV/GPX/KML files within the app itself.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational improvements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
