PrepList: Checklist Maker

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PrepList: Checklist Maker, provided by Chakrit Developer, handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: November 7, 2026
Application ID: com.appme7.preplistchecklistmaker

1. About the app

PrepList: Checklist Maker is an offline-first preparation checklist application designed to help users create, organize, and check off task and packing lists for travel, daily routines, work projects, and life events.

2. Information we access or collect

The application does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All checklist categories, checklist titles, item descriptions, quantities, notes, completion statuses, and reminder schedules are stored exclusively on your device in a local Room SQLite database.

3. How information is used

All information you create is used solely on your device to display, organize, remind, and export your checklists at your explicit direction.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

All data is stored locally within the application's private sandbox on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. When you choose to export your checklists to CSV, the file is saved to the destination you choose via the system file picker or shared through your device's standard share sheet.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The application does not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or analytics services. It operates entirely offline without making external network calls.

  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs are incorporated into this application.

6. Permissions and your choices

The application requests only minimal system permissions required for local operation:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To post reminder alerts when scheduled checklist times arrive (Android 13+).
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: To trigger exact alarms for your checklist preparation deadlines.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To restore your active reminders automatically if your device is restarted.
  • VIBRATE: To provide tactile feedback with reminder notifications.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: To provide optional app locking using your device's secure biometric hardware (fingerprint or face unlock). Biometric verification is performed directly by the Android operating system; raw biometric data is never accessible to the app.

7. Retention and deletion

You retain full control over your data. You can edit or delete any item, checklist, or category at any time directly in the app. Uninstalling the application immediately deletes all local database tables and stored preferences from your device.

8. Children's privacy

The application does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. It is a general utility safe for all age groups.

9. Security

Your data is protected by standard Android operating system sandboxing. In addition, you can activate the optional Biometric Lock feature in Settings to protect your checklists from unauthorized local access.

10. Privacy requests

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, you have immediate, direct access to view, edit, export, and delete your information at any time without submitting a request.

11. Changes to this policy

If we update this privacy policy, changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
