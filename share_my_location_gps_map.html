Share My Location: GPS & Map

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Share My Location: GPS & Map, provided by CKH, handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: 2026-11-26
Application ID: com.gameme7.sharemylocationgpsmap

1. About the app

Share My Location: GPS & Map lets you find or select a position on a map, look up a street address, save a place, and share coordinates or directions. You can use the app without registering for an account. CKH does not operate a server that stores your saved places or a history of your GPS locations.

2. Information we access or collect

With your Android permission, the app uses your approximate or precise location while you use the map, along with the accuracy estimate provided by your device. A place that you save can contain its name, coordinates, address, note and creation time. The app also keeps local settings such as your selected language and theme. If you email CKH, we receive your email address and the information you include.

3. How information is used

The app uses your device location to center the map, show position and accuracy, and calculate straight-line distances to saved places. It uses your chosen coordinates to place a pin and request a street address through the device's Android geocoding service. The device's location or geocoding provider may use its own network service. The app uses the pin, available address and optional note to prepare a message and map links. CKH uses support correspondence to respond to your request.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

The app stores saved places and preferences in private Android app storage. Android backup or device-to-device transfer may copy them, depending on your device and backup settings. Share opens the Android share menu and passes the message to the app you select. Copy puts the message on the system clipboard. A map or directions link sends the selected coordinates to the map service when opened. Your backup provider, receiving apps and link destinations handle their copies under their own privacy terms. Providers may process data in countries other than your own.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The app uses Google Maps SDK for Android and Google Play services for the map and device location functions. Google Maps SDK can transmit request and device metadata, IP address, an SDK identifier, crash diagnostics and map interaction events to Google. When advertising is configured, Google AdMob may collect and share IP address and general location inferred from it, interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. The app does not add selected GPS coordinates, saved places or notes to banner requests. Google User Messaging Platform processes ad consent and privacy options, whose availability depends on Google's configuration and applicable requirements.

  • Google Privacy Policy, including Google Play services
  • Google Maps SDK data disclosure
  • Google AdMob privacy information
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure
  • Android Auto Backup

6. Permissions and your choices

You can limit or revoke location access in Android Settings. You can still select a point on the map or enter coordinates manually. Use Ad privacy choices in the app's Settings when the option is shown. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android's privacy settings, where supported, and manage device backups in Android Settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Saved places and preferences remain in the app's local storage until removed or reset. You can delete a saved place in the app, or use Android's app storage controls or uninstall the app to remove local data. Manage separately any device backups, clipboard contents or messages already sent. CKH keeps support messages for as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve the issue or meet legal obligations. Third-party services apply their own retention policies.

8. Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience. CKH does not knowingly direct it to children under 13 or knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has sent CKH personal information, please contact us so we can address the request.

9. Security

The app uses private Android storage for saved places and preferences. Google documents TLS encryption in transit for the Mobile Ads SDK. Network security for messaging, map links, device backups and device geocoding depends on the service handling that transfer.

10. Privacy requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your data protection authority. Contact CKH about information we hold. You can use Android and in-app controls for data stored by the app and contact the relevant provider about data it processes.

11. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will publish the revised text and its effective date on this page.
