Privacy Commitment Summary: All your tasks, daily planner entries, notes, categories, and completion history are stored 100% locally on your device. We do not operate external accounts or upload your personal tasks to any cloud server.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how To-Do List & Daily Planner ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by CKH, handles your information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Local Task Data: All data that you enter into the application — including task titles, descriptions, due dates, scheduled times, priority levels, categories, and completed items — is stored strictly on your local device using Android SharedPreferences and local storage. No task data is transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Account Registration: The application does not require any account creation, login, password, or profile information to function. You have full offline access to all planning features.

Google AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic information in accordance with user consent.
    Google AdMob Privacy Information

4. User Rights, Data Retention & Deletion

Because all tasks, daily plans, and notes reside solely on your device, you have complete control over your data at all times:

  • You can modify, mark complete, or delete any individual task directly within the application interface.
  • You can permanently remove all stored application data by navigating to Android Settings > Apps > To-Do List & Daily Planner > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the application immediately and permanently removes all stored planner data from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

To-Do List & Daily Planner does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that appropriate steps can be taken.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal requirements. The updated policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
