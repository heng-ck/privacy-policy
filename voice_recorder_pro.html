Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Voice Recorder Pro 3 mobile application (Package: com.appme7.voicerecorder3, hereby referred to as "Application") created by CKH (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an ad-supported free service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Key Summary — 100% Offline-First Local Audio Storage

Voice Recorder Pro 3 is built with an absolute offline-first and privacy-first philosophy. All your audio recordings, voice notes, timestamps, marker flags, equalizer settings, and audio trims are stored 100% locally on your smartphone's storage. Zero audio recordings, microphone data, or personal files are ever uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any external server or cloud database without your explicit manual action (such as tapping the system share button).

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect personally identifiable information. Standard technical diagnostic information may be processed by your mobile operating system when downloading and running the Application, such as:

  • Your device model, manufacturer, and Android operating system version;
  • Internet Protocol (IP) address for advertising network connectivity;
  • Anonymous application crash reports, frame performance, and session durations to help improve app stability.

The Application does not gather or track precise GPS location data.

Device Permissions

To provide high-quality voice recording, background processing, and playback, the Application requests the following permissions:

  • RECORD_AUDIO: Required to capture microphone input for recording voice memos, meetings, lectures, and musical ideas. Microphone data is encoded directly into local audio files on your device and is never transmitted externally.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE: Required on Android 14+ to maintain uninterrupted recording when you switch to other apps or when your device screen is locked.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display active recording status, timer indicators, and notification controls in the system status bar.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Ensures device processor continuity during active recording to prevent unexpected audio clipping or buffer underruns.
  • VIBRATE: Provides subtle haptic feedback when starting, pausing, stopping recordings, or placing timestamp markers.
  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT & MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Allows audio capture from paired Bluetooth headsets or external microphones when connected.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Enables loading locally saved audio files for in-app playback, audio trimming, and equalizer adjustments.

You have full freedom to customize, update, or revoke your advertising personalization consent at any time via the Settings menu inside the Application.

Data Retention & User Deletion

Because all audio recordings, tags, and settings remain solely within your local device storage, you maintain total ownership and control over data deletion. You can permanently delete any or all data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual audio recordings directly within the Application's list;
  • Clearing the Application's Storage and Cache via Android Settings > Apps > Voice Recorder Pro 3 > Storage;
  • Uninstalling the Application through standard Android marketplace or device settings.

The Application does not address or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider at ckh.application@gmail.com so appropriate measures can be taken immediately.

Security

The Service Provider is committed to protecting your privacy. All audio files are generated and stored within Android's sandboxed file storage system or user-approved device media storage, shielded by Android OS application isolation security.
