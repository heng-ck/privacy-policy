Art Puzzle: Slide & Solve Game

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Art Puzzle: Slide & Solve Game, provided by Chakrit, handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: November 14, 2026
Application ID: com.gameme7.artpuzzleslidesolvegame

1. About the app

Art Puzzle: Slide & Solve Game is a classical art sliding tile puzzle game. It allows users to reconstruct famous classical public-domain masterpieces across 3x3, 4x4, and 5x5 grid difficulties. The app is completely playable offline and does not require account registration or cloud sign-in.

2. Information we access or collect

We believe in absolute privacy. The application does not collect, record, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, physical location, photos, contacts, or financial details. Gameplay data (such as puzzle completion status, best move counts, stopwatch records, and chosen appearance theme) is saved strictly on your local device storage using Android's private Room SQLite database and SharedPreferences.

3. How information is used

Locally stored game data is used solely to maintain your game progress, calculate personal best scores, and render your selected color palette and language preferences across app sessions. No profile building or algorithmic behavioral tracking occurs.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

All game data remains inside the application's sandboxed local storage on your device. We do not operate remote databases or synchronize your game stats to external servers. Your local data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The app displays non-intrusive banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous device identifiers and usage metrics in accordance with Google's privacy policies to serve advertisements. In regions subject to regulations such as GDPR or CPRA, user consent is managed via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK, and users can review or modify their consent choices at any time in the app's Settings menu.

6. Permissions and your choices

The application only requests standard network-related permissions (android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) necessary for AdMob banner loading. The app does not request runtime dangerous permissions such as Camera, Location, Microphone, Contacts, or File Storage.

7. Retention and deletion

Because all gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device, you retain full control over your data. You can delete all game progress and best records at any time by clearing the application's storage in your device Settings or by uninstalling the application.

8. Children's privacy

Art Puzzle: Slide & Solve Game contains fine art masterpieces in the public domain and is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Security

Your data is protected by the Android operating system's application sandbox, which prevents other applications from accessing Art Puzzle's private database files.
