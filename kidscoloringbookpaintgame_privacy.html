Kids Coloring Book: Paint Game

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Kids Coloring Book: Paint Game, provided by CKH, handles information when you play the game.

Effective date: 2026-11-19
Application ID: com.gameme7.kidscoloringbookpaintgame

1. About the game

Kids Coloring Book: Paint Game is a relaxing ASMR outline-tracing and region color-filling art game for Android devices, distributed via Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store.

2. Local game data and information accessed

Your gameplay progress is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences (kids_coloring_book_prefs), including unlocked coloring pages, last completed page, star ratings, total score, saved region colors, remaining Magic Hint count, unlocked and selected brush styles, and audio/vibration preferences. We do not collect your name, email address, or account credentials, and local game saves are never uploaded to developer servers.

3. How information is used

Local preferences are used solely to preserve your coloring progress, unlocked brushes, and audio/haptic settings across sessions. When online, third-party advertising SDKs process device identifiers and interaction signals to deliver and measure non-intrusive ads and enforce consent choices.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Local game progress stays on your device. Advertising and consent telemetry generated by Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections and processed by Google in accordance with Google's privacy terms.

5. Third-party services and advertising

Kids Coloring Book: Paint Game uses Google AdMob (Zero-Banner Mode B: optional Rewarded Video ads and milestone Interstitial ads only) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for regional consent management (GDPR/EEA/UK). No third-party billing or analytics SDKs are included.

6. Permissions and your privacy choices

The merged app manifest requests only android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (for ad delivery and consent), and android.permission.VIBRATE (for optional tactile feedback, which can be toggled off in Settings). Unused permissions such as camera, microphone, location, and external storage are not requested. Where required by law, you can review or update consent preferences anytime via the in-game Ad Privacy Choices button in Settings or via your Android device Ad Settings.

7. Retention and deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or server-side profiles. You can delete all locally stored game progress at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the game.

8. Children's privacy

Kids Coloring Book: Paint Game is intended for a general audience (ages 13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

9. Security

Local game data is isolated within Android's private app sandbox, and network communications with Google advertising and consent services use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.

10. Privacy requests

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, update, or withdraw consent for advertising identifiers. Use the in-game Ad Privacy Choices option (when displayed in Settings), your device's privacy controls, or contact us directly by email.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Changes take effect when published with an updated effective date at the top of this page.
