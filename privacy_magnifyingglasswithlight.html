Magnifying Glass with Light

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Magnifying Glass with Light, provided by Chakrit H., handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: November 3, 2026
Application ID: com.appme7.magnifyingglass

1. About the app

Magnifying Glass with Light is an Android utility application that turns your mobile device into a digital magnifying glass with an integrated flashlight, smooth zoom controls, freeze frame, specialized high-contrast reading filters, photo snapshot capture, and a local photo gallery.

2. Information we access or collect

We believe strongly in your privacy. The app does not require user registration or account creation and does not collect or transmit personal identifying information. The camera viewfinder is processed directly on your device in real-time. Photos captured with the app are saved strictly to your device's local storage (Pictures/Magnifier). No images, video frames, or audio are ever uploaded to external servers.

3. How information is used

Camera access is used solely to provide live magnification, zoom, focus, and still-photo capture. Flashlight hardware access is used strictly to illuminate reading material in low-light conditions. Local device storage is used exclusively to store snapshots that you intentionally choose to save. Internet access is used only by third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) to deliver banner advertisements and manage privacy consent.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

All images and application settings (such as selected color palette, language, and zoom preferences) are stored locally on your device using Android Scoped Storage and SharedPreferences. We do not operate external databases or cloud servers to store your magnified photos. You maintain complete control over your captured images and may delete them at any time.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The app displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob and its advertising technology partners may collect and process device identifiers, general location data, and app interaction telemetry to serve relevant ads, prevent fraud, and monitor performance in accordance with their privacy policies. In applicable jurisdictions, we provide a User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialogue allowing you to manage personalized advertising preferences at any time in the app settings.

6. Permissions and your choices

The app requests the following system permissions: CAMERA (to display the live viewfinder and capture photos), FLASHLIGHT (to toggle device illumination), VIBRATE (to provide haptic touch feedback), and INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to load AdMob banner ads). You can revoke camera permission at any time in your device's Android System Settings, though doing so will disable magnification functionality.

7. Retention and deletion

Because we do not store your data on remote servers, retention is governed entirely by you. Photos captured with the app remain in your device storage until you delete them using the in-app gallery or your device's file manager. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete photos saved to your public MediaStore directory.

8. Children's privacy

Magnifying Glass with Light does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general-purpose utility suitable for all ages.

9. Security

Because all magnifying processing occurs locally on-device, your photos and camera streams remain completely confidential. All third-party advertising and consent communications with Google servers are transmitted using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS encryption.

10. Privacy requests

Under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have rights regarding your personal information. Since we do not collect personal identifiers or host user accounts, there is no remote personal profile to access or modify. You can review and update your advertising consent preferences at any time via the "Ad privacy choices" option in the app Settings.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted to this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
