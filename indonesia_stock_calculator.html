Ringkasan Utama — 100% Offline & Privasi Data Tersimpan Lokal

Aplikasi ini dirancang dengan arsitektur 100% offline (offline-first). Seluruh data simulasi transaksi saham IDX, harga beli/jual, jumlah lot/lembar, catatan portofolio, dan perhitungan fee bursa disimpan secara lokal di ponsel Anda menggunakan database SQLite Room. Tidak ada data finansial atau data transaksi pribadi yang dikirim ke server pihak ketiga atau komputasi awan (cloud).

Opt-Out Rights & Data Deletion (Hak Penghapusan Data)

Because all stock simulation records and customized stocks are stored strictly within the Application's private database sandbox on your Android device, you have complete control over your data. You can erase all data instantly by:

  • Deleting saved simulations individually within the "Saved List" (Daftar Tersimpan) tab;
  • Clearing the Application's Data and Cache via Android Settings > Apps > Indonesia Stock Calculator > Storage;
  • Uninstalling the Application through standard Android uninstall procedures.
