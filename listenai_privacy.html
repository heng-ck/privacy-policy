ListenAI - Text to Speech

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ListenAI - Text to Speech, provided by CKH, handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: 2026-10-30
Application ID: com.appme7.texttospeech

1. About the app

ListenAI is an AI-powered text-to-speech reader application that transforms documents, PDFs, eBooks (EPUB), Word files (DOCX/DOC), web articles, and plain text into natural, spoken audio for hands-free listening.

2. Information we access or collect

ListenAI is designed with an on-device first architecture. Your imported documents, extracted text, bookmarks, reading progress, and playback preferences are stored exclusively on your device within an internal SQLite database (Room). The app does not upload your personal document library to external cloud servers. When you use the Online Cloud HD voice engine, the active text segment is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS to Google Cloud speech synthesis endpoints solely to generate the spoken audio stream. When you import content from a web URL, an HTTPS request is made to download the public web page content directly to your device for text extraction.

3. How information is used

Information processed by ListenAI is used strictly to: (1) Synthesize speech and play audio narration, (2) Display synchronized transcripts and manage bookmarks, (3) Extract clean readable text from requested web URLs, and (4) Display non-intrusive banner advertisements and manage privacy consent choices via Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Your reading documents, notes, bookmarks, and audio playback history remain stored locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or transfer your personal documents or reading habits to any third party. Diagnostic data and advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) may be processed by Google Mobile Ads SDK in accordance with Google privacy policies.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display non-intrusive banner advertisements, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to comply with European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and other regional privacy laws (GDPR/PDPA). Users can review or reset their advertising consent preferences at any time in the app Settings under "Ad privacy choices".

6. Permissions and your choices

ListenAI requests only permissions necessary for its core reader functionality: (1) Storage and Media permissions (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and below) to allow you to pick and import documents from your device, (2) Internet access to fetch articles from user-entered URLs, stream Cloud HD voices, and load banner ads, (3) Foreground Service with Media Playback type to enable uninterrupted background listening while using other apps or with screen turned off, (4) Post Notifications to show playback transport controls in the notification tray and lock screen, and optional daily reading reminders, (5) Wake Lock to prevent audio stuttering during CPU sleep, and (6) Boot Completed to restore optional reminder alarms after device reboot. You may grant or revoke permissions at any time in Android System Settings.

7. Retention and deletion

ListenAI does not operate user accounts or cloud databases. All user data, bookmarks, and documents persist locally on your device until you choose to delete them. You can delete individual documents inside the app, clear the audio cache in Settings, or permanently erase all app data by uninstalling ListenAI or selecting "Clear Data" in Android application settings.

8. Children's privacy

ListenAI is intended for general audiences. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our services, please contact us so we can investigate and address the matter.

9. Security

We implement reasonable security safeguards to protect user data. All network communications for web extraction, Cloud TTS streaming, and advertising utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Local document storage is protected by the Android application sandboxing model.

10. Privacy requests

Because we do not maintain accounts, identities, or remote databases of your reading activity, you have direct control over your data directly on your device. For questions regarding advertising consent or privacy practices, you may reach out to us at ckh.application@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with a revised Effective Date.
