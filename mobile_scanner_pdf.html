Privacy Policy - Mobile Scanner PDF

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Mobile Scanner PDF" application (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, created by CKH (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is provided "AS IS".


Offline-First & Local Data Storage

The Application is designed with a strict Offline-First architecture. All scanned document images, camera captures, ID card scans, digital signatures, cropped images, and OCR text extractions are processed and saved 100% locally on your device's internal application storage.

The Service Provider does not upload, transfer, host, or back up your document scans or personal files to any remote server or external cloud database. No mandatory account creation or user registration is required to access the full functionality of the Application.


Information Collection and Use

The Application may collect non-personally identifiable technical diagnostic information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Device manufacturer, model, and operating system version
  • Application interaction statistics, error crash logs, and session durations

The Application does not gather or track precise GPS location data of your mobile device.


Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Application utilizes third-party SDK services to support features and display advertising. These third-party providers may collect anonymized advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy

Camera & Storage Permissions

To deliver document scanning and file management capabilities, the Application requests the following permissions:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively to capture physical documents, ID cards, and passports for scanning within the app. Camera preview feeds are processed locally in real-time and are never recorded or transmitted externally.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used to allow you to import images from your device gallery or save exported PDF documents and images to your device storage.

Opt-Out & Data Deletion Rights

You can instantly delete all local document scans, temporary cache files, and app settings by using the built-in "Clean Cache" feature in Settings, by clearing app data via Android Settings, or by uninstalling the Application. Uninstalling the Application permanently removes all locally stored documents from your device.


Children's Privacy

The Service Provider does not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. The Application does not target anyone under 13 years of age. If the Service Provider discovers that a child under 13 has provided personal information, it will be deleted immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider at ckh.application@gmail.com.


Security Commitment

We prioritize the confidentiality and protection of your data. Because all scanned files remain strictly inside your device's isolated application storage sandbox, your documents remain under your sole control.

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The Service Provider will notify users of any modifications by updating this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy regularly for any updates.
