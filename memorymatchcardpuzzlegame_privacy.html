Memory Match: Card Puzzle Game

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Memory Match: Card Puzzle Game, provided by CKH, handles information when you play the game.

Effective date: 2026-11-21
Application ID: com.gameme7.memorymatchcardpuzzlegame

1. About the game

Memory Match: Card Puzzle Game is an offline-capable single-player card matching puzzle game featuring 50 campaign levels, 4 quick-play card modes, and customizable card faces, 8 gold-foil card backs, and 6 table themes.

2. Local game data and information accessed

We do not require account registration and do not collect your name, email address, or personal files. Your gameplay progress—including unlocked levels, stars, best scores, fewest moves, fastest times, Hint/Peek booster counts, card customization choices, and sound/music/vibration settings—is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences (memory_match_prefs).

3. How information is used

Local save data is used strictly to preserve your game progress and preferences between sessions. When you are online, third-party advertising SDKs process technical identifiers to serve, cap, and measure advertisements and manage regional consent.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Local game saves remain on your device and are never uploaded to developer servers. Advertising-related technical data (such as the Google Advertising ID, approximate location/IP-derived region, and ad interaction diagnostics) is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections to Google advertising services as governed by Google's privacy terms.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to display bottom banner ads (inside a dedicated frame separated from the gameplay board), milestone interstitial ads, and user-initiated rewarded video ads, together with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent in applicable regions (such as the EEA and UK).

6. Permissions and your privacy choices

The app requests only the permissions required for gameplay feedback and advertising: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to load ads and consent forms), com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (Advertising ID for AdMob), and VIBRATE (for optional tactile match feedback). Where required by law, you can review or update your ad consent anytime via Ad Privacy Choices in the in-game Settings screen, or reset/limit ad personalization in your Android device settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game progress is retained on your device until you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game. Because we do not operate user accounts or cloud save servers, no developer-held personal data needs separate account deletion.

8. Children's privacy

Memory Match: Card Puzzle Game is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and older) and is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

All network communications for advertising and consent management use standard HTTPS/TLS transport encryption. Local preferences are isolated within Android's private application sandbox.
