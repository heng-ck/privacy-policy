Background Remover - CutOut

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Background Remover - CutOut, provided by CKH, handles information when you use our mobile application.

Effective date: November 7, 2026
Application ID: com.appme7.backgroundremovecutout

1. About the App

Background Remover - CutOut is an on-device image cutout and creative photo studio application available on Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store. It empowers users to automatically isolate foreground subjects, refine edges with precision tools, design stickers, and create multi-layer visual compositions.

2. Information We Access or Process

We maintain a strict commitment to privacy and data minimization:

  • User Photos & Cutouts (100% On-Device): When you select a photo from your gallery or take a picture using your camera, the image is loaded into local sandbox memory. AI subject segmentation (powered by Google ML Kit) runs entirely on your device. Your photos, cutout masks, and creative edits are never transmitted to our servers or any third-party cloud.
  • App Preferences & Drafts: Your chosen app theme, selected language, and working project drafts are saved exclusively in your device's private app storage.
  • Advertising & Diagnostic Identifiers: We integrate Google AdMob and Google Play Services to serve advertisements and verify system compatibility. These SDKs may collect pseudonymous identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and app interaction telemetry to facilitate ad delivery and prevent fraudulent impressions.

3. How Information Is Used

The information processed is used solely to:

  • Perform on-device background removal and photo editing requested by you.
  • Save and restore your project drafts locally so you can continue editing later.
  • Serve non-intrusive banner advertisements in designated reserved display slots.
  • Ensure technical stability, prevent crashes, and monitor application performance.

4. Storage, Sharing and Transfers

We do not operate backend user databases or cloud image storage. All image editing operations, temporary cache files, and saved drafts remain inside the app's local sandbox on your physical device. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal photos or user data to any third party.

5. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Our application incorporates third-party SDKs that process data under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google ML Kit Terms & Privacy

You can manage your advertising preferences and consent choices at any time through the in-app Settings menu (Ad Privacy) or via your Android device system settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

6. Device Permissions and Your Choices

Our application requests only the minimal system permissions required for normal operation:

  • Photo Picker & Camera: We utilize the modern system Android Photo Picker and camera capture intents with temporary URI grants. The app does not request or require broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES).
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively by Google Play Services and AdMob to download the ML Kit on-device model and retrieve ad creatives.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by AdMob to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads according to your consent status.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all draft files and preferences reside solely on your device, you have complete control over their lifecycle:

  • You can delete individual drafts at any time from the in-app Drafts screen.
  • You can delete all application data and cached files through Android Settings > Apps > Background Remover - CutOut > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the application immediately and permanently removes all stored drafts, thumbnails, and settings from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

Background Remover - CutOut is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party SDKs through our app, please contact us immediately.

9. Security

We prioritize the security of your data by keeping all photo processing strictly on your device. Third-party advertising communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have specific privacy rights regarding personal data. Since we do not maintain personal user accounts or identity databases, exercising your right to delete data is achieved directly on your phone as described in Section 7.
