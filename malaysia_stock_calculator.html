Opt-Out Rights & Data Deletion (Hak Pemadaman Data)

Because all stock simulation records and custom counter data remain exclusively within the Application's private database sandbox on your Android device, you retain full sovereignty over your data. You can delete all stored data immediately at any time by:

  • Deleting saved simulations individually via the "Saved List" (Senarai Simpanan) tab (supports swipe-to-delete with Undo);
  • Clearing the Application's Data and Cache via Android Settings > Apps > Malaysia Stock Calculator > Storage;
  • Uninstalling the Application using standard Android device uninstall procedures.
