Torch Light - Small Flashlight

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Torch Light - Small Flashlight, provided by AppMe7, handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: 2026-11-12
Application ID: com.appme7.torchlightsmallflashlight

1. About the app

Torch Light - Small Flashlight is an offline utility application designed to provide instant camera LED flashlight activation, hardware torch brightness control, SOS emergency distress signaling, high-speed strobe flashing, interactive light bulb glow, traffic lights, and multi-color screen illumination on Android smartphones, foldables, and tablets.

2. Information we access or collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, photos, videos, contacts, or location data. Camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA) is requested strictly to control the device rear LED flash hardware via CameraManager.setTorchMode. The camera image stream is never opened, captured, recorded, or transmitted.

3. How information is used

Any local data (such as selected color palette, screen brightness preference, and language selection) is stored entirely on your device via Android SharedPreferences and is used solely to maintain your preferred app settings across sessions.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

We do not operate remote user servers or databases, and we do not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties.

5. Third-party services and advertising

This app displays banner advertisements powered by Google AdMob and uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to handle consent choices in accordance with privacy laws (such as GDPR in the EEA and UK). Google AdMob may process device identifiers and advertising IDs to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

6. Permissions and your choices

You can manage or revoke permissions at any time through your device System Settings. Revoking Camera permission will disable the LED flash torch, while screen-based illumination modes (Screen Light, Light Bulb, Traffic Light) remain fully functional. You can also review or update your ad privacy preferences at any time via the "Ad Choices" button in the app Settings screen.

7. Retention and deletion

Because no personal information is transmitted to or stored on external servers, there is no remote data retention. Uninstalling the application completely deletes all locally stored preferences from your device.

8. Children's privacy

Our app is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

9. Security

We prioritize security by operating locally on the device without cloud storage. Network communication by Google Play Services and AdMob is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.
