Text on Photo Editor

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Text on Photo Editor, provided by Heng App Studio, handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: November 6, 2026
Application ID: com.appme7.textonphotoeditor

1. About the app

Text on Photo Editor is a mobile creative utility designed for adding typography, stylish fonts, custom captions, and graphic overlays to photos and backgrounds. All photo editing, text styling, and image rendering occur entirely on your Android device.

2. Information we access or collect

We believe in absolute privacy for your creative work. The app does not collect personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location. When you choose to insert pictures or load custom fonts, the app accesses those specific files locally using standard Android system pickers without reading your broader media library. For ad serving and user consent, third-party SDKs may process pseudonymous device identifiers as described below.

3. How information is used

Locally selected images and fonts are processed exclusively on-device to render your designs and save your project drafts. Network connections are used solely by Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display advertisements, handle regional privacy consent choices, and maintain ad operational performance.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Your photos, designs, and draft files are saved directly in your device's private sandbox storage and the system picture gallery via Scoped MediaStore. We do not operate external cloud storage, and your images are never uploaded or transferred to our servers or third parties.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The app integrates third-party services provided by Google to support free development through non-intrusive advertisements:

  • Google Play Services & Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP)

6. Permissions and your choices

Text on Photo Editor requests minimal Android permissions:

  • Internet & Network State: Used exclusively to request advertisements and manage consent status.
  • Photo & Document Access: Managed through the secure Android Photo Picker and Storage Access Framework. The app does not request or require broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES).
  • Ad Privacy Choices: You can review or change your personalized advertising preferences at any time from Settings > Ad privacy.

7. Retention and deletion

Because your project data is stored locally on your device, you retain complete ownership. You can delete individual drafts directly within the Drafts screen. Uninstalling the application will permanently remove all app-stored sandbox drafts from your device.

8. Children's privacy

Text on Photo Editor is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

9. Security

All network communications for advertisements and consent utilize modern encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols. Local files reside within sandboxed application directories protected by Android's operating system security boundaries.

10. Privacy requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to inquire about advertising consent options, you may contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect application enhancements or regulatory changes. The latest version will always be accessible at this URL.
