Link Animal - Animal Connect

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Link Animal - Animal Connect, provided by CKH, handles information when you play the game.

Effective date: 2026-11-19
Application ID: com.gameme7.linkanimalanimalconnect

1. About the game

Link Animal - Animal Connect is a single-player Onet pair-matching puzzle game with 500+ Classic levels and endless Survival waves, designed for offline play with optional advertising.

2. Local game data and information accessed

Your gameplay progress (unlocked levels, star ratings, total score, Survival high score, booster counts for Hints, Shuffles, and Reset Timers, and sound, music, and vibration preferences) is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences (link_animal_prefs and link_animal_iap). We do not operate developer game servers or upload your save data.

3. How information is used

Local game data is used solely to preserve your puzzle progress and audio/haptic settings on your device. When you are online and advertisements are enabled, advertising SDKs process device and app-interaction signals for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Gameplay saves remain locally on your device and are not shared by CKH. Third-party advertising and consent SDKs (Google AdMob and Google UMP) may transmit device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, diagnostics, and ad interaction data over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections in accordance with Google's policies and your consent choices.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The game uses Google AdMob (Interstitial ads and optional Rewarded Video ads; zero banner ads) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage regional privacy consent.

6. Permissions and your privacy choices

The app requests only permissions required for gameplay and advertising: android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, and android.permission.VIBRATE. Where required by your region, you can review or update your consent choices at any time via in-game Settings → Ad Privacy Choices, or reset/limit your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Because Link Animal - Animal Connect uses no user accounts or developer cloud servers, you can delete all local game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the game.

8. Children's privacy

Link Animal - Animal Connect is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and older) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or the applicable age of digital consent in your region. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us to request assistance.

9. Security

Network requests made by the Google Mobile Ads and UMP SDKs are protected in transit using HTTPS/TLS encryption, and local saves stay within Android's private app storage sandbox.

10. Privacy requests

You may manage regional ad consent choices directly in-game (Settings → Ad Privacy Choices when applicable), adjust OS-level advertising preferences, or contact CKH with any privacy question.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will update the effective date at the top of this page when changes are posted.
