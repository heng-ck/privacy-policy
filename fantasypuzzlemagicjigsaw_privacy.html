Fantasy Puzzle: Magic Jigsaw

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fantasy Puzzle: Magic Jigsaw, provided by CKH, handles information when you play the game.

Effective date: 2026-11-22
Application ID: com.gameme7.fantasypuzzlemagicjigsaw

1. About the game

Fantasy Puzzle: Magic Jigsaw is an offline-friendly single-player jigsaw puzzle game with 40 fantasy levels across 5 enchanted worlds, customizable piece counts (16, 36, 64, and 100 pieces), free-form board placement, and ad-supported hint boosters.

2. Local game data and information accessed

We do not require account registration and we do not operate developer servers to store personal player profiles. Your unlocked levels, star ratings, best completion times, in-progress puzzle board states, hint balance, and audio/vibration preferences are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences (fantasy_jigsaw_prefs).

3. How information is used

Local game data is used solely to save and restore your puzzle progress and preferences on your device. Third-party advertising SDKs (Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform) process device/advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostics over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections to serve ads, prevent fraud, and manage regional consent preferences.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Your local gameplay saves remain on your device and are never uploaded by us. Advertising and consent data processed by Google AdMob and Google UMP may be transmitted to and processed by Google in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

5. Third-party services and advertising

Fantasy Puzzle: Magic Jigsaw is free and ad-supported using Google AdMob (Menu Banner on non-gameplay screens only, Milestone Interstitials after level completion, and opt-in Rewarded Video ads for extra Magic Hints) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for GDPR/EEA/UK consent:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

6. Permissions and your privacy choices

The app uses only the following Android permissions: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to load ads and consent forms), com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (Google Advertising ID for AdMob), and VIBRATE (optional haptic snap feedback, toggleable in Settings). No camera, microphone, location, or external storage permissions are requested. Where required by regional law, you can review or update your consent via Ad Privacy Choices inside the game's Settings screen, or reset your Advertising ID in Android device settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game saves remain on your device until you clear the app's data in Android Settings or uninstall the game.

8. Children's privacy

Fantasy Puzzle: Magic Jigsaw is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and older) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region).

9. Security

Network communications made by Google AdMob and Google UMP use standard HTTPS/TLS transport encryption. Local saves are stored inside the app's private Android sandbox.
