Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Germany Stock Calculator (Aktienrechner Deutschland) mobile application (hereby referred to as "Application") created by CKH (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an ad-supported free service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Key Summary — 100% Offline Local Data Storage

The Application is designed with a strict offline-first architecture. All stock simulations, share quantities, buy/sell prices, and transaction records are stored 100% locally on your device via SQLite Room Database. Zero trade calculation data, portfolio details, or financial entries are ever uploaded or transmitted to any external server or cloud service.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects minimal technical diagnostic information when you download and use it through standard mobile operating system routines. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • Operating system version and device hardware characteristics
  • App performance diagnostics, crash logs, and time spent on application screens

The Application does not collect, access, or track precise GPS location data of your mobile device.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Application displays non-intrusive banner advertisements to fund ongoing development and maintenance. It utilizes reputable third-party services that may collect anonymized device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR).

Data Disclosure

The Service Provider may disclose automatically collected information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a valid court order, subpoena, or similar statutory legal process;
  • When believed in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect legal rights, user safety, investigate fraud, or respond to official government requests.

Opt-Out Rights & Data Deletion

Because all user-entered portfolio data is stored exclusively in local device memory, you can delete all stored data immediately at any time by:

  • Using the in-app "Delete" button on saved portfolio items;
  • Clearing the Application's Storage and Cache via your Android Device Settings;
  • Uninstalling the Application using standard device uninstall procedures.

The Application does not address and is not intended for children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact the Service Provider at ckh.application@gmail.com for immediate removal.

Security

The Service Provider employs standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard information handled by the Application. Local database files are secured within the Application's private Android sandbox storage.
