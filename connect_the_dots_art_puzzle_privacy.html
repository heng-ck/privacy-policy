Connect The Dots: Art Puzzle

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Connect The Dots: Art Puzzle, provided by CKH, handles information when you play the game.

Effective date: 2026-11-22
Application ID: com.gameme7.connectthedotsartpuzzle

1. About the game

Connect The Dots: Art Puzzle is a free, ad-supported offline dot-to-dot puzzle game with 25 handcrafted levels, an Art Gallery, optional hints, and adaptive support for phones, tablets, and foldable devices. No user account or login is required to play.

2. Local game data and information accessed

Your game progress—including unlocked levels, completed puzzles, star ratings, remaining hint counts, last played level, and sound, music, and vibration preferences—is stored strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences (connect_dots_prefs). We do not operate developer backend servers and never upload your local saves.

3. How information is used

Local preferences are used solely to preserve your gameplay progress and audio/haptic settings across sessions. Network access is used exclusively by the integrated advertising and consent SDKs to serve ads, manage privacy consent, and prevent ad fraud.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Local gameplay progress remains on your device and is not shared with us or third parties. Advertising partners (Google AdMob) may collect and process technical identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, app interaction signals, and crash/diagnostic data over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections in accordance with their privacy policies.

5. Third-party services and advertising

This game uses Google AdMob (displaying a menu-only banner on non-gameplay screens, milestone interstitials, and optional rewarded video ads for hints) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage user consent in applicable regions such as the EEA and UK. No in-app billing or third-party analytics SDKs are bundled.

6. Permissions and your privacy choices

The app requests only minimal permissions required for gameplay and advertising: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (for loading ads and consent forms), AD_ID (Google Advertising ID), and VIBRATE (for optional haptic feedback). Unused permissions such as camera, storage, location, or microphone are not requested. Where required by law, you can review or update your ad consent preferences anytime via Ad Privacy Choices in the in-game Settings screen or through your device's Android Ad Settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Because no user accounts are created, all local game data can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings or uninstalling the game.

8. Children's privacy

Connect The Dots: Art Puzzle is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and older) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

9. Security

All network communications by third-party advertising and consent SDKs use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, while local game saves remain sandboxed inside private app storage.
