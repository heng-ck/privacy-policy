Drive Log: Mileage Tracker

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Drive Log: Mileage Tracker, provided by AppMe7, handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: 2026-11-09
Application ID: com.appme7.drivelogmileagetracker

1. About the app

Drive Log: Mileage Tracker is an offline-first Android vehicle mileage logbook, hybrid GPS and odometer trip tracker, and tax deduction / reimbursement report generator. The app does not require user registration, login credentials, or a cloud account.

2. Information we access or collect

Your trip records, vehicle profiles, odometer readings, saved places, reimbursement rates, theme preferences, and language choices are stored locally on your device. When you enable GPS tracking modes (Start–End Snapshot or Interval Checkpoints), the app accesses foreground location coordinates (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) on your device to calculate driving distance. When banner advertisements are active, the integrated Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDKs may collect device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), coarse IP-based location, app interaction events, and diagnostic performance data.

3. How information is used

On-device mileage, odometer, and coordinate data are used solely to calculate trip distances, detect odometer gaps, recover unfinished trips, and generate CSV or printable HTML mileage reports on your device. Information processed by Google AdMob and UMP is used to request consent, display banner advertisements, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud in accordance with your regional consent choices.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

All mileage logs, saved places, and vehicle records remain in your device's private application storage unless you explicitly export and share a CSV or HTML report using the Android system share sheet. Third-party advertising and consent requests handled by Google AdMob and UMP are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections to Google servers.

5. Third-party services and advertising

Drive Log: Mileage Tracker uses Google AdMob for banner advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for regional consent management. You can review their data practices at the links below:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & AdSense Partner Policy

6. Permissions and your choices

Foreground location permission (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) is optional and requested only when you start or snapshot a GPS-assisted trip; you can deny or revoke location permission at any time and continue using Manual / Odometer Only mode. Network permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) and Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) are used for AdMob banner loading, UMP consent forms, and opening external policy or store links. Where required by law, you can update your advertising consent at any time via Settings → Ad privacy choices.

7. Retention and deletion

Local trip logs, vehicles, and saved places are retained on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual trips directly inside the Logbook tab, or erase all stored application data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

8. Children's privacy

Drive Log: Mileage Tracker is a vehicle logbook and tax/reimbursement utility intended for adult drivers and business users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

9. Security

Your mileage logbook is stored within Android's sandboxed application storage on your device, and all network communications by integrated SDKs use HTTPS/TLS encryption.

10. Privacy requests

Because the developer does not operate an external server storing your trip logs or personal account data, you have direct on-device control to inspect, export (CSV/HTML), or delete your records. For questions or requests regarding ad consent or privacy rights, please contact us using the email address below.
