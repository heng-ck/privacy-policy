Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the "Shopping List" app (hereby referred to as "Application"), package com.appme7.shoppinglist12, for mobile devices, created by CKH (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). The Application provides shopping lists, reminders, a home-screen widget and a sticker collection, and includes banner advertising. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

Your shopping lists, item names, quantities, units, notes, purchase status, reminder settings, item history, preferences and sticker progress are stored locally on your device in the Application's private storage, including SQLite. No account is required, and the Application does not automatically upload your shopping contents to a Service Provider server or synchronize them with other devices. Google Mobile Ads may collect and share the following information when advertising services operate:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address, which may be used to estimate a general location.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as launches, taps and ad views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the app and advertising SDK.
  • Device identifiers, such as advertising ID and app set ID, and applicable account-related identifiers handled by the SDK.

The Application does not request precise device location. Core list management works offline. Advertising may use the internet for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, subject to the applicable configuration and privacy choices.


Sharing, importing and backing up are initiated by you. A text share or exported list file is sent to the app or recipient you select. Backups are saved to the location you choose through Android's file picker, which may be a local or cloud provider. Those recipients and providers handle copies under their own policies. Clipboard text is read when you choose Paste items. Notifications and the home-screen widget may display list names and shopping contents where others can see your device; use your device's notification and lock-screen settings accordingly.

If you email ckh.application@gmail.com, the Service Provider receives your email address, message and any files you choose to send, and uses them to answer your request. Do not send private shopping contents unless needed for support. The Application does not require contact details for ordinary use or enroll users in marketing emails. Where applicable, processing relies on consent for advertising choices that require it, legitimate interests in responding to support and protecting the service, and legal obligations where relevant. The Application uses Google UMP to present applicable ad consent choices; ordinary use is not a substitute for legally required consent.


Third Party Access

The Service Provider does not sell your shopping-list contents or automatically send them to advertisers. Advertising SDK data is not necessarily anonymous and must not be understood as aggregate-only reporting. Google and its advertising services process data under their policies. Their processing, and any sharing or cloud backup you choose, may take place outside your country; Google's linked privacy policy explains its processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards.


The Application uses Google services for advertising and consent. These providers have their own privacy information:

  • Google Play Services / Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob

For information actually received by the Service Provider, such as a support email, disclosure may occur:

  • When required by applicable law or a valid legal request.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect rights or safety, investigate fraud, or address a security incident.
  • Through service providers needed to receive and respond to your inquiry, subject to applicable confidentiality and data-protection requirements; this does not grant access to shopping data that remains only on your device.

Opt-Out Rights

You can change applicable advertising choices through Ad privacy options in the Application's Settings when that option is available, and use Android's advertising ID controls. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal, and changing ad personalization does not necessarily stop all ad-related processing. You may disable reminders in the app or revoke notification and alarm access in device settings. Clear the Application's storage in Android settings or uninstall it to delete its local data and stop its future operation. Delete exported backups and shared copies separately; uninstalling does not erase information already held by recipients or advertising providers. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or port personal data, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data-protection authority. You also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, where applicable. Contact ckh.application@gmail.com to exercise rights concerning information held by the Service Provider; Google-held data is also subject to Google's own privacy controls.


Data Retention Policy

Local shopping data remains on your device until changed, cleared or removed with the Application. Deleting a list or item from the interface may leave related suggestion history, earned stickers or local technical records; clearing app storage removes the Application's local dataset. Android automatic app-data backup is disabled, but files you export remain wherever you save or share them until you delete them there. The Service Provider cannot remotely access or erase shopping data stored only on your device. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal or security needs; contact ckh.application@gmail.com for deletion requests. Advertising providers apply their own retention and deletion policies, as described in their linked privacy information.


Children

The Application is intended for general users aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly solicit personal information from, or market the Application to, children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the Service Provider, contact ckh.application@gmail.com so the information can be investigated and deleted where appropriate. A parent or guardian can remove locally stored information by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Any higher age or parental-consent requirements under applicable local law still apply.


Security

The Application uses Android's private app storage for its local database. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Exported JSON files are not password-protected or encrypted by the Application; protect them and share them only with intended recipients. Protect your device with its available security controls. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.
