Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Netherlands Stock Calculator (Aandelen Rekenmachine Nederland) mobile application (hereby referred to as "Application") created by CKH (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an ad-supported free service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Key Summary — 100% Offline Local Data Storage

The Application is built upon a privacy-first, offline architecture. All Euronext Amsterdam calculations, share purchases, sell scenarios, and trade records are stored 100% locally on your smartphone using SQLite Room Database. Zero trade details, personal finances, or portfolio simulations are ever transmitted to any external server or third-party cloud.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects minimal technical diagnostic information through standard Android operating system APIs when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Operating system version and device hardware specifications
  • App performance diagnostics and anonymized crash reporting

The Application does not collect, record, or track precise geographical location data.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Application displays non-intrusive banner advertisements to support ongoing development. It utilizes industry-standard third-party SDKs that may process pseudonymized device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) in strict compliance with applicable privacy regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR / AVG in the Netherlands).

GDPR (AVG) Consent Management

In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Algemene Verordening Gegevensbescherming - AVG), users in the Netherlands and European Economic Area (EEA) are presented with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog upon initial launch. You retain full control to manage or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time via the "Reset Consent" option in the Application's Settings menu.

Data Disclosure

The Service Provider may disclose automatically collected diagnostic information only when required by law (such as to comply with a court order, subpoena, or lawful governmental inquiry), or when necessary in good faith to protect rights, prevent fraud, or ensure user safety.

Opt-Out Rights & Data Deletion

Because all user portfolio entries and calculation inputs remain strictly on your local device, you can permanently erase all stored data at any time by:

  • Deleting saved position cards directly within the Application's History tab;
  • Clearing the Application's Data and Cache via Android Settings > Apps > Netherlands Stock Calculator > Storage;
  • Uninstalling the Application using standard device uninstall procedures.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not address and is not directed towards children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact ckh.application@gmail.com for immediate deletion.

Security

The Service Provider provides standard procedural, electronic, and physical safeguards to protect information processed by the Application. Local database records are protected by Android's application sandbox security boundary.
