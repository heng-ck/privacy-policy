Paint by Number: Coloring Book

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Paint by Number: Coloring Book, provided by CKH, handles information when you play the game.

Effective date: 2026-11-19
Application ID: com.gameme7.paintbynumbercoloringbook

1. About the game

Paint by Number: Coloring Book is a free-to-play, offline-capable digital coloring book and color-by-number puzzle game featuring 60 curated artworks, pinch-to-zoom vector canvases, Hint and Magic Wand boosters, and animated Time-Lapse replays.

2. Local game data and information accessed

All gameplay progress — including unlocked levels, per-artwork region coloring states, completed artworks, total score, star ratings, remaining Hint and Magic Wand booster counts, and sound, music, and vibration preferences — is stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences (paint_by_number_prefs). The developer does not operate user accounts or upload your game save files to external servers. Third-party advertising SDKs integrated in the game (Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform) may automatically access your Android Advertising ID, coarse IP-based location, app interactions (ad impressions and clicks), and crash/diagnostic telemetry.

3. How information is used

Local save data is used solely to preserve your coloring progress and audio/haptic settings across sessions. Data processed by Google AdMob and UMP is used to serve milestone Interstitial and optional Rewarded Video advertisements, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and honor your regional privacy consent choices.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Your coloring progress remains stored strictly on your device. Advertising and consent telemetry processed by Google AdMob and UMP is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS in accordance with Google's data processing and international transfer practices.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The game uses a Zero-Banner (Mode B) ad model with milestone Interstitial ads and optional Rewarded Video ads (to earn extra Hints or Magic Wands), managed through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). No Google Play Billing or external analytics SDKs are compiled.

6. Permissions and your privacy choices

The merged release manifest declares only android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (for AdMob/UMP), and android.permission.VIBRATE (for optional haptic feedback toggleable in Settings). The game does not request camera, microphone, storage, contacts, or location permissions. Where required by GDPR/EEA/UK regulations, an Ad Privacy Choices option is displayed inside the in-game Settings screen so you can review or withdraw consent anytime. You may also reset or opt out of personalized ads via Android System Settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

7. Retention and deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall Paint by Number: Coloring Book, which permanently deletes all SharedPreferences save files.

8. Children's privacy

Paint by Number: Coloring Book is intended for a general audience (ages 13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

9. Security

Local progress is isolated in Android's private app storage, and third-party ad/consent communications use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

10. Privacy requests

Because we do not maintain user accounts or collect personal profiles on developer servers, you can delete all local game data directly on your device or manage ad consent through the in-game Ad Privacy Choices button (when available) or contact us by email.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
