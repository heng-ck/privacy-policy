Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the "Voice Recorder" app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by the Service Provider as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed to help you record and manage audio recordings. Most of the data processed is stored locally on your device. This includes:

  • Audio Recordings: Voice and sound recordings created by you using the microphone feature.
  • File Metadata: Filenames, creation dates, sizes, and durations of your recordings.
  • Settings: Your preferences for app language and theme color.

In addition, the Application may collect certain information automatically, such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (IP address)
  • The operating system and device model you use
  • Basic usage analytics (e.g., features used, time spent in the app) to help us improve the service.

The Application does not gather precise real-time information about the location of your mobile device. The Application does not upload your audio recordings to any external server; your recordings remain private to your device unless you choose to share them manually via other apps.

Permissions Required

To provide its core functionality, the Application requests the following permissions:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): To allow the app to capture and record audio.
  • Storage (READ/WRITE): To save your recordings to the device and load them for playback.
  • Internet & Advertising ID: To show relevant advertisements through AdMob and monitor app stability.

Third Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services to aid the Service Provider in improving the Application. The Application utilizes third-party services that have their own Privacy Policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (Advertising)

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain User Provided data (stored on your device) for as long as you use the Application. Since your recordings are stored locally, deleting the app or the files manually will typically remove this data. If you have questions about data handling, please contact us at ckh.application@gmail.com.

Children

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If a child under 13 has provided personal information, the Service Provider will immediately delete this from their servers. If you are a parent and aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Security

We are committed to safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. We provide procedural safeguards to protect the information we process and maintain on your device.
