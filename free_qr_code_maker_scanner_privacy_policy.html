Free QR Code Maker & Scanner

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Free QR Code Maker & Scanner, provided by CKH, handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: 2026-11-24
Application ID: com.appme7.freeqrcodemakerscanner

1. About the app

Free QR Code Maker & Scanner lets you create and style QR codes (website links, Wi-Fi, contact cards, text, email, SMS, phone, social profiles, calendar events and map locations), scan QR codes and barcodes with the camera or from an image, and extract web links from printed text with the camera (OCR). The app has no user accounts and requires no registration. It is free and supported by ads.

2. Information we access or collect

CKH, the developer, does not collect, receive or store your personal information on its own servers. The app does not run developer servers and does not upload your QR content, camera images, recognized text or history.

The app processes the following on your device only:

  • Content you enter or scan: QR code content (for example links, Wi-Fi network names and passwords, contact details, messages, event details or locations), labels, design settings and saved templates.
  • Camera: live camera frames, used only while the QR scanner or Camera OCR screen is open to detect codes and text. Frames are analysed on the device and are not saved unless you choose to keep a captured result.
  • Images you choose: pictures you pick to scan, run OCR on, or use as a QR centre logo. The app receives access only to the images you select through the system picker; for a logo it stores a reference to the image you chose, not a copy.
  • History and preferences: your created QR codes, scan and OCR history, favourites, theme, language and settings.

Third-party services built into the app (Google AdMob, Google's User Messaging Platform and Google ML Kit, described in section 5) may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions and diagnostics under their own policies.

3. How information is used

Information on your device is used only to provide the app's features: generating and previewing QR codes, scanning and recognizing codes and text, checking scanned links for warning signs (done entirely on the device), and showing your saved QR codes, templates and history. Information collected by Google's services is used by Google to show and measure ads, request and store your consent choices, prevent fraud, and maintain and improve the on-device recognition features.

4. Storage, sharing and transfers

Your QR codes, templates, history and settings are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. CKH does not sell or share this content. Information leaves the app only when you choose to: for example when you share or print a QR code, save a QR image to your gallery, copy content to the clipboard, or open a scanned link, phone number, email or map in another app.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup stored in your own Google account, as controlled by your device's backup settings. CKH has no access to these backups.

5. Third-party services and advertising

The app uses the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob shows banner ads. It may collect the advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions and diagnostics to serve, personalize (where you allow it) and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for and stores your ad consent choices where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). You can review or change these choices at any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices when that option is shown.
  • Google ML Kit (barcode scanning and text recognition) runs on your device. Camera images and recognized text are processed on the device. ML Kit may contact Google servers to download or update models and to send usage and performance metrics about the recognition features.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

6. Permissions and your choices

  • Camera: requested only when you open the QR scanner or Camera OCR. If you deny it, you can still create QR codes and scan images from your gallery.
  • Internet and network state: used to load ads and for the Google services above.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described in section 5.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone or files. Gallery images are opened only through the system picker for the specific images you choose.

7. Retention and deletion

Saved QR codes and templates stay on your device until you delete them. Scan history is deleted automatically after the period chosen in Settings > Scan history auto-delete (365 days by default), except entries you mark to keep or star as favourites. You can delete individual items in the app, delete old scans at any time, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data. Data held by Google's services is retained under Google's policies linked above.

8. Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. CKH does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

9. Security

Your app data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. Google's services transmit their data using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Because QR codes can contain sensitive details such as Wi-Fi passwords, take care when sharing or printing them and keep your device protected with a screen lock.

10. Privacy requests

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. Because CKH does not hold your app content on its servers, you control that data directly on your device as described in section 7. For data processed by Google's services, use the controls and links in section 5. For any privacy question or request, email ckh.application@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the services it uses change. The updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
